Bilal Best Buys - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

1. Introduction

Bilal Best Buys provides tools to help manage eBay seller activities through eBay's authorized APIs.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions you approve, our application may access eBay seller account information, inventory and listing information needed for its features.

We do not ask for or store your eBay password.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to connect your eBay seller account and provide the seller-management features you authorize.

4. Data Storage and Security

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored using Cloudflare Workers KV. We take reasonable steps to protect stored information, but no online storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Sharing

Information may be processed by eBay and Cloudflare as necessary to operate the application. We do not sell personal information.

6. Access and Revocation

You can revoke the application's access through your eBay account. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us by email.

7. Third-Party Services

This application uses eBay APIs and Cloudflare services, which have their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change.
